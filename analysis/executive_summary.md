# Executive Summary

**Dataset:** DMart store_daily (synthetic practice data), five stores, 450 store-days
**Period:** 2026-04-01 to 2026-06-29
**Author:** AMAN

## Headline finding
Pune's low revenue is a footfall gap, not a stockout gap.

## Magnitude
Pune's mean daily revenue is 26.14% below the all-store mean, and its footfall is
25.00% below the all-store mean footfall. Revenue per footfall is only 1.52% below
(Pune 419.29 against 425.75 for all stores).

## What is driving it and what is not
Driver: fewer visitors. Revenue moves closely with footfall (correlation 0.918 across
all rows, 0.735 inside Pune). Not a driver: stockouts. Pune ranks 4th of 5 by mean
stockout hours (2.32 against 2.74), and the revenue-stockout correlation is weak
(-0.152 across all rows). Correlation does not establish causation.

## Supporting facts
- Pune is in the Poor band on 70 of the 71 Poor days across all stores.
- Hyderabad has the most stockout hours (3.85) yet earns 893233.01 a day, far above Pune.
- Pune's mean footfall is 1517.71 against 2023.59 for all stores.

## Open hypotheses
 X: Unconfirmed: Pune's catchment area is smaller. Data needed: population and household data around the store.
Y: Unconfirmed: nearby competitors pull customers away. Data needed: locations and footfall of competitor stores.
Z: Unconfirmed: local marketing brings fewer visitors. Data needed: marketing spend and visitor counts by channel.
